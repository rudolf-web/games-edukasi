📘 Concept Review

Radius (r): from the centre to the edge. Diameter (d): across the circle through the centre. d = 2 × r, so r = d ÷ 2. (jari-jari, diameter)

Circumference (C): the distance around the circle. C = π × d = 2 × π × r. (keliling)

Area (A): the space inside the circle. A = π × r × r = πr². Use the radius, not the diameter! (luas)

In this game, π = 3.14. Units: circumference in cm, area in cm².
