📘 Concept Review

Multiple: a number you get by multiplying by a whole number. Multiples of 4: 4, 8, 12, 16… (kelipatan)

Factor: a number that divides exactly into another. Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. (faktor)

LCM (Lowest Common Multiple): the smallest number that is a multiple of both. LCM of 4 and 6 = 12. (KPK)

HCF (Highest Common Factor): the biggest number that is a factor of both. HCF of 12 and 18 = 6. (FPB)

Tip: a factor is always ≤ the number; a multiple is always ≥ the number.
