How to Play

  1. Tap a balloon to answer. No aiming, no dragging.
  2. A wrong balloon pops grey and stays out — keep trying until you get it right.
  3. First try: 100 points plus a combo bonus (up to 200). Second try: 50 points. Third try or later: 25 points.
  4. Answer three questions in a row on the first try to enter FEVER MODE — faster beat and a balloon frenzy.
  5. There is no timer. Take your time and think.
  6. 10 questions in 2 stages: Balloon Park, then Sky Festival.
