Multiples — Kelipatan

A multiple of a number is the result of multiplying it by 1, 2, 3, 4, … Multiples go on forever and a number is always a multiple of itself.

Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, …
5th multiple of 6 = 5 × 6 = 30

Factors — Faktor

A factor divides a number exactly, with no remainder. Factors always come in pairs that multiply to the number.

Factors of 24: 1×24, 2×12, 3×8, 4×6
1234681224

HCF — Highest Common Factor (FPB)

The HCF is the largest factor shared by two or more numbers. Using prime factorisation, take the smallest power of each common prime.

12 = 2² × 3  •  18 = 2 × 3²
HCF of 12 and 18 = 2 × 3 = 6

LCM — Lowest Common Multiple (KPK)

The LCM is the smallest multiple shared by two or more numbers. Using prime factorisation, take the largest power of each prime.

6 = 2 × 3  •  8 = 2³
LCM of 6 and 8 = 2³ × 3 = 24
