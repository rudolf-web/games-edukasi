1. Expressions & Equations

Simplify by collecting like terms (same letter, same power).

3(a − 4) + 2a = 3a − 12 + 2a = 5a − 12

To solve an equation, do the same thing to both sides.

Example: 3x − 7 = 14 → 3x = 21 → x = 7

2. Factors, Multiples, HCF & LCM

  • Factor: a number that divides exactly. Factors of 84 include 12 (84 ÷ 12 = 7).
  • Prime factorisation: write as a product of primes. 72 = 2³ × 3².
  • HCF: the largest common factor. HCF of 36 and 54 = 18.
  • LCM: the smallest common multiple. LCM of 12 and 18 = 36.

3. Data: Mean, Median, Mode & Charts

  • Mean = sum of all values ÷ number of values.
  • Median = the middle value when data is in order.
  • Mode = the value that appears most often (highest frequency).
  • Line graph is best for showing change over time (e.g. temperature over a week).
Example: Mean of 5, 7, 9, 11, 13 = 45 ÷ 5 = 9

4. Three-Dimensional Shapes

Cuboid: V = length × width × height
Cube: V = s³  •  Surface area = 6s²
Cylinder: V = πr²h  •  Circle: A = πr²
  • Cube: 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices.
  • Triangular prism: 5 faces, 9 edges, 6 vertices.
Example: Cuboid 8 × 5 × 3 → V = 120 cm³. Cube with surface area 294 cm² → 6s² = 294 → s = 7 cm.

5. Probability & Complementary Events

P(event) = favourable outcomes ÷ total outcomes
P(A′) = 1 − P(A)
  • Die greater than 4: numbers 5, 6 → 2/6 = 1/3.
  • Bag with 4 red, 6 blue: P(red) = 4/10 = 2/5.
  • Two coins, at least one head: HH, HT, TH → 3/4.
  • If P(A) = 0.3, then P(A′) = 1 − 0.3 = 0.7.

6. Quadratic Expressions & Perimeter

  • Quadratic expression: highest power is x². Example: x² − 4x + 3.
  • Perimeter of a rectangle = 2(length + width).
Example: Perimeter 50 cm, length (2x + 5) cm, width (x − 3) cm → 2(2x + 5 + x − 3) = 50.
